"Who is Hiring?" postings near 12-year lows as AI reaches half of all jobs (November 2026)

November 2026 Hacker News Hiring Trends

November Highlights

Saturday, November 14, 2026

It's been a while since the last update, so this one covers a lot of ground. As of November 14, November's thread has 244 job postings, down from 391 a year ago. Every thread since July has landed between 224 and 255 postings, and August's 224 was the fewest since January 2014. So far 2026 averages 287 postings a month, the lowest yearly average since 2013.

At the same time, AI is now mentioned in more than half of all postings (51.6%), up from 30% two years ago.

AI is in half of all postings

AI appears in 51.6% of November's postings, second only to Remote. It crossed 50% for the first time in June 2026. Two years ago it was 30%.

The work itself is shifting toward agents. AI agents (including "agentic") appear in 14.8% of postings, ahead of LLMs at 10.7%. MCP barely existed a year ago (0.3%) and is now in 4.5% of postings, the biggest climb in this month's rankings. Meanwhile, the older machine learning label slipped from 14.8% to 11.5%.

Top 10 Programming Languages

Python (22.1%) and TypeScript (17.6%) still lead, though TypeScript is down from 22.5% a year ago. Rust has climbed to #3, appearing in 8.2% of postings, up from 4.9%, and has passed Go for the first time. C++ and Java are up too, but on small numbers (14 and 11 postings).

Fewer generalist and senior roles

Postings asking for generalists are down sharply from a year ago: Full Stack fell from 27.4% to 19.3%, Back End from 23.8% to 18.4%, and Front End from 13.0% to 7.4%. Senior and Staff titles are down as well (30.7% to 26.6%, and 13.0% to 9.0%). Junior went the other way, from 1.8% to 4.5%, though that's only 11 postings.

Compare Full Stack, Back End, Front End, and Senior

Remote, Onsite, and Hybrid

Work arrangements have barely moved. Remote is in 55.7% of postings, Onsite 26.2%, and Hybrid 17.2%, all within two points of last November.

Compare Remote, Onsite, and Hybrid

Changes to the data

New terms this month: AI Agents, LLM, MCP, RAG, LangChain, Anthropic, pgvector, Vector Database, Bun and Deno. Every thread back to 2011 is re-analyzed on each run, so their full history is included.

I also fixed a bug where a few terms with alternate spellings didn't count their own name (for example, "Next.js" was only counted when written "NextJS"). Next.js, NestJS, WebAssembly, Front End and Back End are higher across all months than in earlier posts. The December 2011 thread, which has a single posting, is no longer included.
